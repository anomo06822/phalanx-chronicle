# First Session Playtest Brief

## Demo Path

Cold launch -> Chapter I briefing -> Battle of Guangzong -> battle result -> war camp next-step CTA.

## Target Personas

- New player: verify they can start, move, attack, wait, and end turn without live coaching.
- Strategy player: verify attack, skill, and threat outcomes are readable before committing.
- Collaborator or stakeholder: verify the product pitch is clear from intro, first battle, and return-to-camp flow.

## Facilitator Notes

- Let the tester play without narration unless they are hard blocked for more than 30 seconds.
- Ask them to think aloud when they hesitate on a click or a menu decision.
- Record the moment they enter the first battle, finish the first full turn, and choose the next action in camp.

## Feedback Prompts

1. What did you think the game wanted you to do first?
2. Before you clicked Attack or Skill, did the HUD make the result clear enough?
3. After the battle ended, did the war camp make the next recommended action obvious?

## Success Criteria

- 4 of 5 testers enter the first battle within 1 minute.
- 4 of 5 testers complete the first full turn without coaching.
- 4 of 5 testers can explain the next recommended step after returning to camp.
